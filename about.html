About EcoGlow Skincare

EcoGlow Skincare is a fictional boutique South African skincare brand founded in 2022 in Cape Town. Our brand was created to celebrate natural beauty while promoting environmentally responsible skincare.

Our Mission

To empower individuals to care for their skin using sustainable, ethically harvested African botanicals. We aim to create skincare products that are gentle on the skin and respectful of the environment.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading eco-conscious skincare brand by 2028 while inspiring people to make more sustainable choices in their daily lives.

Our Values

Natural Beauty

We celebrate natural ingredients and simple skincare routines.

Sustainability

We believe in environmentally responsible packaging and production.

Ethical Sourcing

We support responsible sourcing of African botanical ingredients.
